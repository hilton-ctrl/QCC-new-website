# **Market Psychology Report: Carpet Cleaning & Pest Control (Australia)**

This analysis is based on:

1. Hundreds of reviews from your uploaded review file covering multiple Australian carpet cleaning and pest control businesses.  
2. Australian Reddit discussions about carpet cleaning, bond cleaning, end-of-lease requirements, DIY vs professional cleaning, and provider complaints.  
3. Public review platforms showing both positive and negative customer experiences.

What follows is not merely sentiment analysis.

This is a psychological analysis of what people are really buying.

---

# **The Core Discovery**

Most customers think they are buying:

* Carpet cleaning  
* Pest control  
* Stain removal

But psychologically they are buying:

* Relief  
* Peace of mind  
* Pride  
* Protection from judgment  
* Protection from financial loss  
* Restoration of control

The carpet is often just the visible symptom.

The emotional outcome is the real product.
