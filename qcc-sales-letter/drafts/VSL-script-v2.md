## THE SCRIPT

**[HOOK — pattern interrupt + open loop #1]**
Before you book *any* carpet cleaner on the Gold Coast — there's one question you need to ask them.
Most can't answer it. And the ones who dodge it are the exact ones who'll cost you.
Give me two minutes and I'll give you the question — plus the three-step system two local blokes built so you'd never get burned by a carpet cleaner again.

**[RECOGNITION — pacing their reality]**
If you've hired a cleaner before, you already know the script.
The phone quote sounds great... until the van's in your driveway and suddenly there are "extras."
The carpet looks good for a fortnight... then the stains creep back and the pet smell returns like it never left.
Or they turn up late, rush the job in forty minutes, and they're gone before you've even checked the back bedrooms.
And here's what that really costs you.
It's not just the money.
It's standing at your own front door feeling embarrassed of your own home.
It's the quiet panic about what guests can smell.
It's an inspection coming up — and thousands of dollars of bond riding on carpet you're not sure about.

**[THE REAL PROBLEM — "not your fault" + enemy]**
Now, here's the part nobody tells you.
When a carpet clean fails — it's almost never the carpet. It's who's holding the wand.
From the outside, every carpet cleaner looks identical. Same vans. Same promises. Same "satisfaction guaranteed."
So the cowboys hide in plain sight.
They win the job with a cheap number, blast every stain with the same soapy water — even though pet urine, red wine and traffic lanes each need a completely different treatment — soak your carpet so it stays damp for days... and then stop answering the phone.
You couldn't have spotted them in advance. Nobody can. That's the trap.
Which brings me back to that question I promised you. *(open loop #2 held)*

**[THE MECHANISM — hope + belief]**
We're Mike and Jack — Quick Carpet Cleaners. Two owners, one van, Gold Coast born and bred.
And because we'd heard the same horror stories from every second customer, we built our entire service around a simple idea: make getting burned *impossible*.
We call it the Walk-Through Guarantee Clean. Three steps.
**Step one — the Locked Quote.** You get an itemised price before we arrive. The number on the quote is the number on the invoice. Anything unexpected, we tell you first — and you decide.
**Step two — the stain-matched clean, done by the owners.** Mike or Jack personally inspects every stain and matches the treatment to it. Enzyme breakdown for pet accidents — so the smell is destroyed at the source, not perfumed over. Specialist spotters for wine, coffee and rust. Deep extraction with controlled moisture for the traffic lanes — so your carpet is walkable the same day. Not three days. The same day.
**Step three — and this is the one that changes everything — the Walk-Through Sign-Off.** Before we pack a single hose, you walk every room with us. If anything's not right, we fix it on the spot. The job isn't finished when *we* say so. It's finished when *you* say so.

**[PROOF]**

**[THE OFFER]**
So here's what a visit looks like.
We call it the Fresh Home Reset.
A full stain-matched deep clean through up to ❓[X] rooms. Enzyme and deodorise treatment for pet odours. High-traffic-lane restoration. Same-day walkable finish. And the walk-through sign-off before we leave.
One locked price: ❓[$XXX].
To put that in perspective — re-carpeting a single lounge room runs past three thousand dollars. And a lost bond costs even more.
❓ [Bonus stack if client confirms: e.g. "Book this week and we'll also apply a fabric stain-shield, free."]

**[RISK REVERSAL]**
And you carry zero risk.
You approve every room before we drive away — that's built in.
If a treated stain reappears within ❓[fourteen] days, we come back and re-treat it free.
And on an end-of-lease clean, if your property manager flags the carpets, we re-clean the flagged areas for nothing. ❓[client to confirm]
If we can't earn your sign-off, we haven't finished the job.

**[PAYOFF THE HOOK — close loop #1]**
Oh — and that question you should ask any cleaner before you book?
It's this: *"Will you walk every room with me before you leave — and fix anything I'm not happy with, on the spot?"*
Ask it. Watch them squirm.
We're the ones who put it in writing.

**[CROSSROADS CLOSE + CTA]**
So you're standing at a fork in the road.
One path — you close this video and keep living with the smell, the stains, and the quiet dread every time someone knocks on your door.
The other path — one phone call, a locked quote in two minutes, and this time next week you're walking barefoot on carpet that looks, feels and smells brand new. In a home you're proud of again.
There are only two of us — that's the whole point — so the calendar genuinely fills.
If your inspection date is set, or guests are coming, don't leave it.
Tap the button, or call Mike and Jack now, and lock in your spot.
Your home — and your bond — will thank you.
