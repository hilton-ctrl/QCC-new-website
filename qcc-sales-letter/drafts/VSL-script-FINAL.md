**[HOOK — pattern interrupt + open loop #1]**
Before you book *any* carpet cleaner on the Gold Coast — there's one question you need to ask them.

Give me two minutes and I'll give you the question — plus the three-step system two local blokes built so you'd never get burned by a carpet cleaner again.

If you've hired a cleaner before, you already know the script.
The phone quote sounds great... until the van's in your driveway and suddenly there are "extras."
The carpet looks good for a fortnight... then the stains creep back and the pet smell returns like it never left.

It's standing at your own front door feeling embarrassed of your own home.
It's the quiet panic about what guests can smell.
It's an inspection coming up — and thousands of dollars of bond riding on carpet you're not sure about.

**[THE REAL PROBLEM — "not your fault" + enemy]**
Now, here's the part nobody tells you.
When a carpet clean fails — it's almost never the carpet. It's who's holding the wand.
From the outside, every carpet cleaner looks identical. Same vans. Same promises. Same "satisfaction guaranteed."
So the cowboys hide in plain sight.
They win the job with a cheap number, blast every stain with the same soapy water — even though pet urine, red wine and traffic lanes each need a completely different treatment — soak your carpet so it stays damp for days... and then stop answering the phone.
You couldn't have spotted them in advance. Nobody can. That's the trap.
Which brings me back to that question I promised you. *(open loop #2 held)*

**[THE MECHANISM — hope + belief]**
We're Michael and Jack — Quick Carpet Cleaners. Two owners, one van, Gold Coast born and bred.
And because we'd heard the same horror stories from every second customer, we built our entire service around a simple idea: make getting burned *impossible*.
We call it the Walk-Through Guarantee Clean. Three steps.
**Step one — the Locked Quote.** You get an itemised price before we arrive. The number on the quote is the number on the invoice. Anything unexpected, we tell you first — and you decide.
**Step two — the stain-matched clean, done by the owners.** Michael or Jack personally inspects every stain and matches the treatment to it. Enzyme breakdown for pet accidents — so the smell is broken down in the pile, not perfumed over. And if it's soaked deeper than the carpet, you'll hear that from me straight, before you pay. Specialist spotters for wine, coffee and rust. Deep extraction with controlled moisture for the traffic lanes — so your carpet is walkable the same day. Not three days. The same day.
**Step three — and this is the one that changes everything — the Walk-Through Sign-Off.** Before we pack a single hose, you walk every room with us. If anything's not right, we fix it on the spot. The job isn't finished when *we* say so. It's finished when *you* say so.

**[PROOF]**
That system is why, after nine years and more than four thousand cleans across the northern Gold Coast, we're sitting on ninety-seven five-star Google reviews. Not four point something. Five.
Here's just one of them.
A local mum had make-up and foundation ground into three rooms of carpet. Everyone told her it was permanent — she'd already priced up *replacing* it.
One clean later, she went straight to Google and wrote — quote — "I had make up stains and all sorts. Very impressed with the results. Wouldn't recommend anyone else."
That carpet she was about to rip out? Still on her floor. Looking new.
And John, facing a bond inspection on short notice, put it even simpler: "Punctual, clean, efficient — ticked every box for the landlord."

**[THE OFFER]**
So here's what a visit looks like.
We call it the Fresh Home Reset.
A full stain-matched deep clean through up to three rooms. Enzyme and deodorise treatment for pet odours. High-traffic-lane restoration. Same-day walkable finish. And the walk-through sign-off before we leave.
One locked price: three hundred and thirty dollars.
To put that in perspective — re-carpeting your home runs well past five and a half thousand. And a lost bond costs thousands more. This is a small fraction of that — and you approve the result before we drive away.

**[RISK REVERSAL]**
Now — I'm not going to stand here and hand you a guarantee.
And I'll tell you exactly why, because it's the same reason you should trust the rest of it.
A promise to come back is worth nothing if coming back can't fix the problem. When a carpet doesn't lift on the day, it isn't dirty anymore — it's damaged. A second visit won't change that. The blokes who promise you one either don't know that, or they're banking on you not calling.
So we put everything into getting it right the first time. And then we let the record speak.
Nine years. Over four thousand cleans. Not one carpet we've cleaned has ever been flagged by a property manager. Not one.
You approve every room before we leave — that's built in.
And if a stain won't lift, or that smell is coming from the underlay instead of the carpet, you hear it from us before you pay. Never after.
If we can't earn your sign-off, we haven't finished the job.

**[PAYOFF THE HOOK — close loop #1]**
Oh — and that question you should ask any cleaner before you book?
It's this: *"Will you walk every room with me before you leave — and fix anything I'm not happy with, on the spot?"*
Ask it. Watch them squirm.
We're the ones who put it in writing.

Call Michael and Jack now — that's zero four eight four... three one two... nine six six — and lock in your spot.
