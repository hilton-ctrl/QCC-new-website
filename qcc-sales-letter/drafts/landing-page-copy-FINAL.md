## FAQ (accordion — feeds FAQPage schema)

1. **How long until I can walk on the carpets?** You can walk on it the moment we finish — but stay off it until it's dry if you can. Damp carpet picks dirt straight back up, so whatever's on your soles goes into the pile you just paid to have cleaned. Most rooms are dry in a few hours with the windows open.
2. **Will you really remove pet smells, not just mask them?** If the urine is in the carpet pile, yes — enzyme treatment breaks it down and extraction lifts it out. But urine soaks downward. Once it reaches the backing or the underlay, that's underneath the carpet, and cleaning the surface can't fix a smell coming from below it. So we clean properly first, you give it a week, then we assess what's actually left. No guessing, and no promises we can't keep.
3. **Is the quote really fixed?** The number on your quote is the number on your invoice. Anything unexpected gets discussed with you first, and you decide.
4. **Do you do end of lease / bond carpet cleaning?** Yes — with an itemised receipt for your property manager. In nine years and more than 4,000 cleans, not one carpet we've cleaned has been flagged by a property manager.
5. **Are you insured?** Fully insured, owner-operated, Gold Coast local.
6. **What if a stain is genuinely permanent?** We have to try it before we can call it. That judgement comes from nine years of spot and stain chemistry, not a guess from the doorway — so we give it our best shot, and you hear the verdict before we charge you, never after.
