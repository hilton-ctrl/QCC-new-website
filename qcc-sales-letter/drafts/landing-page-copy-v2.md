**H2: The Problem Isn't Carpet Cleaning. It's Who's Holding the Wand.**

- **The bait-and-switch quote.** A cheap number gets them in the door; the "extras" appear once the van's parked.

Then they drive off, and *you're* the one left explaining the smell to your guests — or the stains to your property manager.
