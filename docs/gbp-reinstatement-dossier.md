## 6a. Service-area vs. displayed address — decision 2026-09-09

**Decision: remain a service-area business. Do not display the street address.**

The ranking question is real — location-based profiles generally outrank
service-area profiles in the Map Pack, sometimes by a wide margin. But:

1. **Hiding the address does not remove the location pin.** Google still
   calculates distance from the hidden address, not from the service-area list.
   Upper Coomera sits centrally among the eight declared suburbs, so the
   proximity signal still works. What is given up is the display, not the
   geography.
2. **QCC is not eligible to display it.** Hiding is mandatory for any business
   that does not serve customers at its location. Nobody attends 111/1 Sauvignon
   Parade for a carpet clean.
3. **The address is shared with Odette's Baby Bump 3D 4D** (§5). Displaying it
   would publicly show two businesses at one address — on a profile already
   actioned once.

Displaying the address would trade a ranking uplift the business cannot
legitimately claim against the permanent loss of 97 reviews. Not close.
Revisit only if the business ever opens genuine customer-facing premises.
